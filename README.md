# Almost-equidistant sets and triangle-free graphs

**Hyunjun Yi, Bryan Zachary Bu, Karam Gill**

September 2026, revised October 2026. Accepted for JMM 2027 (AMS Contributed Paper Session on Graph Theory).

DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23661262](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23661262)

## Abstract

A set of points in ℝᵈ is almost-equidistant if among any three of them some two are at distance 1. The pairs at non-unit distance form a triangle-free graph, and we study f(d), the largest such set in ℝᵈ, through that graph in both directions. Constructions from the Hoffman–Singleton, Gewirtz, M22 and Higman–Sims graphs give f(10) ≥ 26 and f(22) ≥ 100 and beat the previous record 2d + 4 for every 20 ≤ d ≤ 57. On the upper-bound side, an almost-equidistant set with no two points closer than 1 has at most (d+1)^{4/3} + 6(d+1) points.
